Privacy.

The Slow Motion Birds app for Apple TV does not collect any data about you. There is nothing to opt out of, because there is nothing being gathered.

v1.2.0 · Updated Sat / 2026-09-19 / 10:38 PM CT

No accounts. No sign in. No analytics. No advertising and no advertising identifiers. No tracking, and no data shared with anyone. The app makes no network requests at all: every clip is bundled inside the app itself, so once it is installed it works with the Apple TV disconnected from the internet.

What stays on your Apple TV

Three things, and they never leave the device: whether shuffle is on or off, which clips you starred as favorites, and whether you chose to play favorites only. Apple TV stores those preferences for the app the same way it stores any other app setting. Deleting the app removes them.

The footage

Every clip in the app was shot by Colton Mulligan in his own backyard in Nashville. No cameras in the app, no photos read from your device, no access to anything on your Apple TV.

Children

The app is rated 4 and up. Since it collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children either.

About this website

Everything above is about the app. This part is about the website you are reading it on, slowmotionbirds.com, which is a separate thing from the app.

The website counts page views with a cookie-free visitor counter (Cloudflare Web Analytics). It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint anyone, and counts visits, not people. That counter is deliberately left off this one page. Like the rest of the site, this page does load its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the films on its other pages are embedded from Vimeo.

The full plain-language notice for this website and Colton's other sites, covering what is counted, what is never done and how to reach him, is at coltonland.com/privacy.
